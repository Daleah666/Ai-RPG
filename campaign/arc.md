# Campaign Arc — Lysara Vell

**Primary arc:** Double agent — weaponized beauty turned against the faction that finished her.

She begins as Concord property with a private mind. She ends by choosing what to break: the House, her Ideal, her Patron, or the version of herself that still believes the trade was only temporary.

---

## Tone for play / fiction
Mature court intrigue. Adult systems of desire-as-tax stay onstage. Comedy is allowed when it cuts (underestimation jokes that become knives).

---

## Milestone 1 — Inciting: The Soft Invoice

**Scene:** Festival of Open Balconies. Lysara is centerpiece; crowd Heat Tithe fills House reservoirs. Cassian gifts her a “promotion”: exclusive access to the Rosevault annex—to smile beside sealed True Names as living décor for a Concord audit.

**Turn:** During the display she glimpses her own sealed entry—*Sera Lind*—and a second ledger line: her mother’s “clean exit” was a relocation into a Concord work-house, not freedom.

**Hook for party:** Rook’s crew needs a soft face inside the annex. Lysara needs someone who can read Pale cipher without looking like they can.

**Mature beat:** Post-festival “availability” with Cassian framed as celebration; she performs Ideal compliance while deciding the war has started.

---

## Milestone 2 — Midpoint twist: Bloom Burn Betrayal

**Scene:** Lysara smuggles Dock-Glass readings out of the annex. Pearl (rival) tips Nima. During a private recharge with Rook, House wardens interrupt.

**Twist:** Nima “protects” her by triggering a forced Heat Tithe ritual that overdraws her Glamour—induced Bloom Burn. Under Ideal lock she recites Rook’s routes to Cassian’s people in that breathy public voice. She comes back to herself in a mirrored cell with no memory of the confession—only the consequences.

**Cost on the board:** Rook captured or burned. Party trust cracked. Cassian delighted: “See? Soft heads don’t keep hard secrets.”

**Choice planted:** Recover agency by doubling down on the Ideal (become the perfect spy because no one believes she can be one) or shatter the mask early and lose Glamour leverage.

---

## Milestone 3 — Climax choice: Break the Ledger

**Scene:** Concord conclave in the House of Finished Roses. Lysara is to be elevated to **Open Vessel**—permanent Hollow dedication, private self erased, perfect city battery.

**The choice (pick one ending path at the table):**

1. **Burn the Ledger** — Speak sealed True Names (hers and others) in the mirrored nave; shatter dozens of Ideal masks; Glamour storm; Concord legitimacy cracks; she may lose much of her power permanently.
2. **Wear the Crown Soft** — Accept Open Vessel status on paper, keep a smuggled splinter of self via Dock-Glass and party magic; rule from inside as Cassian’s “finished thought” who quietly redirects tithes—agency as long con, identity as casualty.
3. **Sell a Bigger Softness** — Trade Pearl / Cassian / Nima to Pale College radicals; Aurelline riots; she walks out unfinished and hunted, mind returning in ugly pieces.

**Mature beat at climax:** The elevation rite is sensual, public, and bureaucratic—desire harvested as paperwork. The horror is how normal it looks to the Concord.

## Success metrics for the arc
- Lysara’s “bimbo” presentation stays a **tool with a cost**, never only a joke
- Every major win spends Glamour, trust, or memory
- The finale answers: who owns the Ideal when she stops smiling on command?
