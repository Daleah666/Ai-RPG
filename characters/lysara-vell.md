# Lysara Vell — Character Bible

**Court name:** Lysara Vell (soft name on the Ledger)  
**Sealed True Name:** Sera Lind (held in the Rosevault; she half-remembers the sound)  
**Role:** Idealist vessel / Concord soft-weapon  
**Party role:** Face, infiltrator, chaos magnet

---

## 1. Hook
Looks like a vacant rose in silk—then she quotes your secret back to you in the same breathy tone she uses for wine orders.

**Image:** platinum-rose hair, mirrored lacquer nails, a laugh that arrives a half-second late.  
**Contradiction:** performed emptiness vs. a private ledger of every useful whisper she has ever “forgotten.”

---

## 2. Origin
Sera Lind was a Pale College copyist’s daughter—ink-stained, sharp, broke. At nineteen she sold herself into the House of Finished Roses to erase a family debt and buy her mother a clean license out of Aurelline’s tax cages.

The finishing was mostly voluntary. The parts that were not (memory shearing, True Name sealing, voice redesign) she signed for under perfume and pressure. She told herself it was strategy. Some days that is still true.

---

## 3. Public face
- Voice: soft, breathy, slightly behind the conversation—on purpose
- Style: Concord pinks and mirror-thread; jewelry that doubles as Glamour foci
- How others treat her: decoration with a schedule; Patrons speak over her; servants envy and resent her; enemies underestimate her until too late
- Catchphrase energy: agrees too easily, asks “dumb” clarifying questions that force people to confess details

---

## 4. Private truth
**Wants:** enough power to own her contract—or burn the Ledger that holds it. Not purity. Not “getting smart again” as a moral arc. Agency with the body she paid for.

**Hides:** she still reads. Smuggled primers. Dock-side cipher. She practices looking like she is not practicing.

**Cannot hide:** Bloom Burn tells on her. When overdrawn, the Ideal locks—giggles, compliance, hunger for eyes—and her private self goes dark for hours.

## 6. D&D skeleton (light 5e-flavored)

- **Lineage:** Human (Aurelline-finished) — optional trait: *Mirror-Touched*: advantage on Charisma (Deception) while maintaining Ideal performance; disadvantage on Intelligence checks made in public mirrored wards.
- **Class sketch:** Bard (College of Glamour-adjacent) / custom Idealist dip — primary stats Cha > Dex > Con; Int deliberately low *on the sheet the Concord sees*; private play can track a hidden “mask Int” vs “true Int” if the table likes that friction.
- **Background:** Court Idealist (custom)
  - Feature **Soft Access:** entrance to Concord festivals, bathhouses, and Patron galleries as décor; servants answer airy questions they would refuse from armed strangers.
- **Alignment-as-attitude:** Chaotic Neutral leaning—loyal to her own survival and selected people, not to House sermons.

### Three signature abilities

1. **Vacant Smile (enchantment).** As you perform Ideal vacancy, one creature that can see your face has disadvantage on Insight against you, and you may cast a charm-adjacent effect (table: *charm person* / *suggestion* scale). **Cost:** after use, make a Hollow check; failure loses a minor personal memory until long rest in unmirrored space.
2. **Heat Tithe.** When you are the focus of open desire or crowd attention, regain a spell slot or Bardic Inspiration die. **Cost:** Concord clause—if licensed handlers are present, half the surge is metered to the House. Unlicensed use is a crime.
3. **Cracked Mirror (reaction).** When your True Name is spoken or you drop Ideal performance mid-spell, you may expend the backlash as a psychic shockwave (force/psychic damage + brief truth-telling aura). **Cost:** you are stunned by memory flood for 1 round; Ideal mask will not hold until you complete a short rest ritual.

### Magic items (2)

1. **Ornelle Contract Band** — gold-rose collar-ring. Advantage on saves vs. fear while worn; Cassian can *message* her 1/day; removing it without ritual alerts House wards.
2. **Dock-Glass Compact** — Rook’s gift. A cracked compact mirror that shows True Names as faint watermark text when held in shadow. 3 charges/day; overuse triggers Bloom Burn.

## 7. Mature beats (story function, not filler)

Use adult content when it reveals **power, vulnerability, or status**:

- Sponsorship “availability” clauses as horror and leverage—not titillation alone
- Lysara using desired-body performance to extract secrets, then hating the Hollow’s cut afterward
- Intimacy with Rook as the rare place she is allowed complexity—and the danger of being seen without the Ideal
- Cassian’s affection as ownership theater; sex as invoice
- Festival climaxes where public desire is literally harvested—her body as city infrastructure

Consent note for tables: discuss lines/veils. The setting contains coercion systems; play can center resistance, negotiation, and aftermath without requiring graphic scenes.

## Short bio (usable blurb)

Lysara Vell is Aurelline’s favorite finished rose: a Concord Idealist who traded sharpness for Glamour to clear a debt and stayed sharp in secret. Patron Cassian wears her like a perfume. Handler Nima sells her hours. Smuggler Rook keeps calling her Sera. Between festivals she smiles for the Bright Mirror and quietly plans who will own the Ledger when the mask finally cracks.
