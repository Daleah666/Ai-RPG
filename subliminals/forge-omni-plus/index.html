# Sleep deeper every night The first # line is what it's for; the AI writes toward it. Other # lines are notes. @foundation silent The lines under it go in that layer: foundation (when this happens, I do this), current (why I care) or surface (who I am becoming). Each plays silent (on a high carrier you can't make out), masked (very quiet under the noise) or aloud. @set speed=2 layers=6 Sets the sound, the same way the repo's audio maker reads it. It moves into the controls on the right. Drop a text file here to add its lines, or an Omni settings file to load it.
