    Instructions for the AI
    This is everything the AI is told about how to work. Edit it freely. It is sent with every Make AI do it button.
    How it works
    1. You type the kind of subliminal in step 1 of the Audio tab.
    2. You press Make AI do it on one section, or Make AI do everything.
    3. The AI gets four things: these instructions, your subliminal type, your current settings, and the job for that one section.
    4. It sends back new settings and its reasoning. Forge checks every value is in a safe range, then fills the section in.
    5. Its reasoning shows under the section as "AI's view", so you can see why it chose what it did.
    6. It only touches the section you asked for. Everything else stays as it was.
