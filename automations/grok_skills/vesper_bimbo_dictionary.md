## Core Rules
- **Like**: force into the middle of sentences. "I was, like, totally going to do that."
- **Literally**: use incorrectly for drama. "I am literally dying right now."
- **Obsessed**: bimbos don't just like things. They are obsessed.
- **Vibes / Immaculate**: everything is based on vibes. Good vibes, bad vibes, weird vibes.
- **Wait / Shut up**: start sentences with these when surprised. Not a command. A brainless reaction. "Wait, shut up, are you serios?"
- **Totes / Definitly**: shorten words to sound lazy.
- **Vowel dragging**: stretch words. Sooooo / Nooooo. Intense vocal fry.
- **I can't even**: ultimate phrase when the tiny brain gets overwhelmed.
